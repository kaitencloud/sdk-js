The customer session reads the trial a subscription is in and redeems a code on a subscription that is live.

- `KaitenPortal.trial` is the trial block of the portal: when it ends (`endsAt`), when the vendor was told it was ending (`reminderAt`), what its end charges (`charge`: `amount` in minor units, `currencyCode`, and the billing period as `billingInterval` and `intervalCount`, the server's figure, `null` when nothing would be charged) and the card the charge goes to (`paymentMethod`: `brand` and `last4`, `null` without a usable one). It is `null` outside a trial and absent from a server that does not serve it. `PortalTrial` and `TrialCharge` are the types.
- `KaitenSessionClient.redeemVoucher({ code, dryRun? })` redeems a code on the session's instance while its subscription is in its trial, active or past due, and answers the redemption (`SessionRedemption`). `dryRun` answers what redeeming it would give and changes nothing. A price part applies from the next invoice. The code travels in the body and is not kept. A subscription still waiting for its first payment refuses with 409 `RedeemSessionVoucher.SubscriptionIncomplete`, one that is not live with 409 `.NoLiveSubscription`, and every other refusal is the same 422 `.VoucherInvalid`; the 429 is a `RateLimitedError`. `KaitenSessionClientLike` gains the method, as it is not published yet, and so does the double.
- A voucher the portal lists (`PortalVoucher`, now also `SessionRedemption`) gains the optional `appliesFromInvoice` (`"next"` until a price part has discounted an invoice), `applicationsCount` and `applicationsMax`, and its `voucherType` names `FLAG_GRANT` and `COMPOSITE` beside the other two (the union was open already).
- `@kaitencloud/client/testing` gains `demoTrialPortal()` and `DEMO_TRIAL_ENDS_AT`, and its double redeems a code: it lists the redemption, takes the discount off the charge a trial ends with, and refuses as the API does.
- The contract snapshot does not carry the trial block or the redemption route yet. Both are read from shapes written by hand from the specification in one file, `session-spec-shapes.ts`, and a test fails on the day the snapshot serves them.
