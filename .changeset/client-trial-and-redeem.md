The customer session reads the trial a subscription is in and redeems a code on a subscription that is live.

- `KaitenPortal.trial` is the trial block of the portal, read from the contract's `PortalTrial`: when it ends (`endsAt`), when the `trial_will_end` event is, or was, due (`reminderAt`, `null` when the API gives none), what its end charges (`charge`: `amount` in minor units, `currencyCode`, and the billing period of the charge as `billingInterval` and `intervalCount`, the server's figure, `null` when the API says nothing would be charged) and the card the charge goes to (`paymentMethod`: `brand` and `last4`, either `null` when the provider gives none; the whole member `null` without a usable card). It is `null` outside a trial and absent from a server that predates the block. `PortalTrial` and `TrialCharge` are the types. The contract makes the portal's `charge` a required member, so `PortalTrial.charge` is `TrialCharge | null`, no longer optional, and the client trusts it: a server that leaves it out is not conforming, and its trial reads as `charge: null`; `reminderAt` and the two labels of the card are nullable. The period is the charge's own, no longer the subscription's, and the second name the specification's documents gave the charge is gone.
- `KaitenSessionClient.redeemVoucher({ code, dryRun? })` redeems a code on the session's instance while its subscription is in its trial, active or past due (`POST /public/session/vouchers/redeem`), and answers the redemption (`SessionRedemption`, the contract's). `dryRun` answers what redeeming it would give and changes nothing; the route answers it with a 200 and a redemption made with a 201, the same body, and the client takes both. A price part applies from the next invoice. The code travels in the body and is not kept, and nothing is sent again after a failure. The refusals of the route reach the host as `KaitenError`s read by their `code`: 409 `RedeemSessionVoucher.SubscriptionIncomplete` (a checkout still waits for its payment) and `.NoLiveSubscription`, 422 `.InstanceRequired` (a session bound to the customer alone) and `.VoucherInvalid`, which is the one answer to every refusal of the code, whatever the reason, and says nothing of it. A 429 `RedeemSessionVoucher.RateLimited` (10 attempts in 10 minutes per session and 30 an hour per customer, `validateVoucher` included) is a `RateLimitedError` with the `Retry-After`; a bare 429 from a gateway is a plain `KaitenError` that says so by its `status`. `KaitenSessionClientLike` gains the method, as it is not published yet, and so does the double, which also refuses a session bound to the customer alone.
- A voucher the portal lists (`PortalVoucher`, now also `SessionRedemption`) gains `appliesFromInvoice` (`"next"` until a price part has discounted an invoice, `null` otherwise), `applicationsCount` and `applicationsMax` (`null` without a price part, or when it never ends), and its `voucherType` names `FLAG_GRANT` and `COMPOSITE` beside the other two (the union was open already). The contract makes the three required, and the portal's `vouchers` and a redemption are one `SessionRedemption`; the SDK's `PortalVoucher` makes them required too, as the customer session client is not in a published release and no 1.0 host builds one, and the client trusts the contract: a server that leaves one out is not conforming.
- `@kaitencloud/client/testing` gains `demoTrialPortal()` and `DEMO_TRIAL_ENDS_AT`, and its double redeems a code: it lists the redemption, takes the discount off the charge a trial ends with, and refuses as the API does. `demoPortal()` says `trial: null`, as a server that serves the block does outside a trial.
