The customer session reads the trial a subscription is in and redeems a code on a subscription that is live.

- `KaitenPortal.trial` is the trial block of the portal, read from the contract's `PortalTrial`: when it ends (`endsAt`), when the vendor is told (`reminderAt`, `null` when no reminder is due), what its end charges (`charge`: `amount` in minor units, `currencyCode`, and the billing period of the charge as `billingInterval` and `intervalCount`, the server's figure, `null` when the API says nothing would be charged) and the card the charge goes to (`paymentMethod`: `brand` and `last4`, either `null` when the provider gives none; the whole member `null` without a usable card). It is `null` outside a trial and absent from a server that predates the block. `PortalTrial` and `TrialCharge` are the types. The portal's `charge` is always there, so `PortalTrial.charge` is `TrialCharge | null`, no longer optional; `reminderAt` and the two labels of the card are nullable. The period is the charge's own, no longer the subscription's, and the second name the specification's documents gave the charge is gone.
- `KaitenSessionClient.redeemVoucher({ code, dryRun? })` redeems a code on the session's instance while its subscription is in its trial, active or past due, and answers the redemption (`SessionRedemption`). `dryRun` answers what redeeming it would give and changes nothing. A price part applies from the next invoice. The code travels in the body and is not kept. A subscription still waiting for its first payment refuses with 409 `RedeemSessionVoucher.SubscriptionIncomplete`, one that is not live with 409 `.NoLiveSubscription`, and every other refusal is the same 422 `.VoucherInvalid`; a 429 with the API's `*.RateLimited` code is a `RateLimitedError`, and a bare 429 from a gateway a plain `KaitenError` that says so by its `status`. `KaitenSessionClientLike` gains the method, as it is not published yet, and so does the double.
- A voucher the portal lists (`PortalVoucher`, now also `SessionRedemption`) gains the optional `appliesFromInvoice` (`"next"` until a price part has discounted an invoice), `applicationsCount` and `applicationsMax`, and its `voucherType` names `FLAG_GRANT` and `COMPOSITE` beside the other two (the union was open already).
- `@kaitencloud/client/testing` gains `demoTrialPortal()` and `DEMO_TRIAL_ENDS_AT`, and its double redeems a code: it lists the redemption, takes the discount off the charge a trial ends with, and refuses as the API does. `demoPortal()` says `trial: null`, as a server that serves the block does outside a trial.
- The contract snapshot does not carry the redemption route (`POST /public/session/vouchers/redeem`) yet. It is read from a shape written by hand from the specification in one file, `session-spec-shapes.ts`, and a test fails on the day the snapshot serves it.
