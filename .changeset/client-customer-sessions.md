`KaitenSessionClient`: what a browser does for one signed-in customer, through the `/public/session/*` routes of Kaiten servers that ship billing, with a `kst_` customer session the vendor's backend mints.

- `new KaitenSessionClient({ apiUrl, getCustomerSession })` takes a host callback returning `{ token, expiresAt }`. The session is sent as `Authorization: Bearer kst_…` to the session routes and nowhere else, with no cookie; it is renewed 60 seconds before it expires, by one call however many requests wait, and on a 401 renewed once and the request retried once before `onAuthError` is told.
- Its calls: `getPortal`, `listInvoices`, `checkout`, `cancelSubscription`, `reactivateSubscription`, `createPortalSession`, `createPaymentMethodSession`, `completePaymentMethodSession`, `validateVoucher` and `setAddonQuantity`, with their types (`KaitenPortal`, `PortalSubscription`, `SessionInvoice`, `CheckoutInput`, `CheckoutResult`, `PortalSession`, `PaymentMethodSession`, `CompletedPaymentMethodSession`, `VoucherValidation`, `SessionAddon`, …). Money follows the SDK's conventions: `currencyCode`, integer minor units, totals from the server only. Reads are retried after a network failure or a 5xx, writes never, and every refusal keeps the API's `status`, `code` and `retryAfterSeconds`.
- The answers are read through the types Kaiten's contract generates, so their shapes are the contract's: a session bound to the customer only lists its `instances` and has no `instance`; a payment method's labels, an invoice's `issuedAt`, `dueAt` and `paidAt`, a discount line's `unitAmountDecimal` and a trial's end are `null` when the API has none; a voucher's `duration` is `ONE_TIME`, `REPEATING` or `FOREVER`, and a discount's value and a boost's modifier are decimal strings; the billing portal link is a `url` and nothing else; an entitlement's `provenance` carries the identifiers of the rows each layer comes from. A checkout is one of a preview, a payment method to save first, or a subscription started, each with the members its status carries. A subscription's `status`, and the `billingStatus` of a customer's instances, name `INCOMPLETE`, a checkout waiting for its first payment, beside `TRIAL`, `ACTIVE`, `PAST_DUE` and `CANCELED`.
- `KaitenSessionClientLike` is its interface. `KaitenClientLike` gains no member, so 1.0 doubles stay valid.
- `ResolvedEntitlement` gains an optional `provenance`, set on the portal's effective entitlements.
